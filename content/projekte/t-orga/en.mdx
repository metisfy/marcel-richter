A metisCode project, part of the metisOne group. My role: concept and development.

## The project

T-Orga is software for businesses that work outdoors – landscaping, caretaking, painting and renovation. Calendar, jobs, time tracking, photos, quotes and invoices come together in one system, in the office on a computer and on site on a phone.

Behind it are an area for the business, a customer portal and a separate admin for database and storage – with two-factor sign-in, invoices in ZUGFeRD format and a setup that runs entirely in the browser.
