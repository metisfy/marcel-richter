A project of the metisOne group, delivered by metisCare. My role: overall responsibility as managing director of metisOne.

## The project

metisNatal is the group's first own product: a digital midwifery centre operated by metisCare. Services are recorded, checked by AI and billed in a structured way to the right health insurer.

The platform is being built together with practising midwives – close to everyday work, with data protection as the foundation, not an add-on.
