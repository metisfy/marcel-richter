A metisCode project, part of the metisOne group. My role: concept and development.

## The project

For Campingplatz Bürgel in Offenbach am Main we built a calm, clear website: pitches for motorhomes, caravans and tents, prices, boat ramp and directions.

A multi-step enquiry wizard collects everything needed for a reservation – validated in the browser and on the server.
