My personal website, with a design based on [metisone.de](https://metisone.de). My role: concept
and development.

## What it does

- Fixtures, results, league table and my appearances are refreshed hourly from the Bavarian
  Volleyball Association, plus a page with statistics and a report for every matchday
- German and English, light and dark theme, accessible to WCAG AA
- Lighthouse 96–100 in every category, no cookies, no tracking

## Tech

Next.js (App Router) with statically generated pages that refresh hourly in the background.
Projects and texts live as MDX and YAML files in the repository. Every push to `main` is
checked, built into a Docker image and published to the GitHub Container Registry.
