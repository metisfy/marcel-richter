## Background

Training times lived in a WhatsApp group, fixtures on the association's website, and changes
regularly got lost. The goal was a single place where players, coaches and parents find
everything that matters.

## Implementation

- Progressive web app that installs like a native app
- Fixtures are imported automatically from the association's data
- Push notifications when training is cancelled or the pitch changes

<Figure src="/images/fotos/training-02.jpg" alt="Team warming up (placeholder image)" caption="Example of an embedded image with a caption." />

## Outcome

After three months, more than 80 % of active members use the app, and coordination within the
coaching team has become much easier.
