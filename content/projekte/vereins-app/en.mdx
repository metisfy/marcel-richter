## Background

Training times lived in a WhatsApp group, fixtures on the association's website, and when a
gym was suddenly unavailable, half the team never heard about it. The app brings all of that
together for players, coaches and parents.

## Implementation

- Progressive web app that installs like a native app
- Fixtures are imported automatically from the association's data
- Push notification when training is cancelled or moves to another gym

## Outcome

After three months, more than 80 % of active members use the app. Gym changes now reach
everyone.
