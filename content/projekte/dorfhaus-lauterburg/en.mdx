## The project

For the Förderverein Kindergarten im Dorfhaus Lauterburg e. V. we built a website around the village hall: events calendar, lunch menu, gallery, hall rental and membership.

With its own admin area the association maintains events, lunch menu, gallery and users itself – no technical knowledge needed.
