A project of the metisOne group, delivered by metisCode. My role: overall responsibility as managing director of metisOne.

## The project

The remuneration AI checks every recorded service against the applicable remuneration agreement: it detects missing details, implausible combinations and typical sources of error before a claim leaves the practice.

It suggests, classifies and warns – but doesn't decide in place of the professional. Professional control stays with people; the AI makes them faster and more confident.
