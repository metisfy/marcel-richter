A project of the metisOne group, delivered by metisCode. My role: concept and development.

## The project

metisBrain brings scattered knowledge together in one place and makes it accessible through a single question. Instead of searching, you ask – and get the answer with its source.

The product is taking shape: we're building AI-powered search, a knowledge graph and the platform around it. Still in progress, but with a clear direction.
