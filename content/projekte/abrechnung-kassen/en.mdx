A project of the metisOne group, delivered by metisCare. My role: overall responsibility as managing director of metisOne.

## The project

The billing module sends checked services in a structured way to the right health insurer – with clear interfaces and a traceable review workflow.

Instead of forms and paper, midwives can see at any time what is open, submitted and paid. The foundation for running metisNatal.
