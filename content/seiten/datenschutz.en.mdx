## 3. Hosting and server log files

The website runs on servers operated by metisOne UG (haftungsbeschränkt) in Germany. With every
request, your browser automatically transmits data that the server records in log files:

- IP address of the requesting device
- date and time of access
- requested page or file and amount of data transferred
- previously visited page (referrer)
- browser type, browser version and operating system

This data is technically necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure. The legal basis
is our legitimate interest under Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Log files are deleted after 14 days at
the latest unless they are needed longer to investigate a security incident.

Fixtures, results, the league table and match reports on the "Season" page are fetched by our
server regularly (at most once an hour) from the public website of the Bavarian Volleyball
Association (volleyball.bayern). On match days the live score comes from the association's live
ticker (bvv.sams-ticker.de): your browser only asks our server, which fetches the data from the
ticker. We show the starting line-ups with shirt number, first and last name of the players and the
coaches, as published by the association in its live ticker and on its match pages. The legal
basis is our legitimate interest in reporting on league play (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Anyone who does
not want to be named can write to us at any time (section 1).
No data about you is passed on; your browser does not connect to the association.

## 4. Cloudflare

All requests to this website pass through the network of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St.,
San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare delivers the site, protects it against attacks and
overload and provides the encrypted connection. In doing so, Cloudflare processes the technical
connection data listed in section 3, in particular your IP address.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the website securely and reliably
(Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). We have concluded a data processing agreement with Cloudflare under
Art. 28 GDPR. Data may be transferred to the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data
Privacy Framework, and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses apply in addition.
Only if Cloudflare suspects an attack may it set a strictly necessary security cookie
(Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG). More information:
[cloudflare.com/privacypolicy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## 5. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies, neither its own nor third-party cookies (for the exception in
case of a suspected attack, see section 4). That is why there is no cookie banner.

If you switch between the light and dark design using the toggle in the header, your browser
stores this choice in its local storage (key "theme", value "light" or "dark"). It stays on
your device and is not sent to us. The legal basis is Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, as storing it
is strictly necessary for the function you requested. You can delete the entry at any time in
your browser settings.
