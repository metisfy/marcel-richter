## 3. Hosting and server log files

{/* CHECK before going live: if a service such as Cloudflare (proxy or tunnel) sits in front of
    the server, or it runs at an external host, it must be named here – see the German version. */}

The website runs on servers operated by metisOne UG (haftungsbeschränkt) in Germany. With every
request, your browser automatically transmits data that the server records in log files:

- IP address of the requesting device
- date and time of access
- requested page or file and amount of data transferred
- previously visited page (referrer)
- browser type, browser version and operating system

This data is technically necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure. The legal basis
is our legitimate interest under Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Log files are deleted after 14 days at
the latest unless they are needed longer to investigate a security incident.

## 4. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies, neither its own nor third-party cookies. That is why there is no
cookie banner.

If you switch between the light and dark design using the toggle in the header, your browser
stores this choice in its local storage (key "theme", value "light" or "dark"). It stays on
your device and is not sent to us. The legal basis is Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG, as storing it
is strictly necessary for the function you requested. You can delete the entry at any time in
your browser settings.

## 5. Contact by email or phone

If you contact us or Marcel Richter via one of the email addresses given on this website or by
phone, we process your details only to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b)
GDPR where your enquiry relates to a contract, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. We delete the
data once it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention periods apply.

of them. Their privacy policy applies there.

## 7. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- access (Art. 15 GDPR)
- rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
- erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
- restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- withdrawal of consent (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)
- objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

An informal message to [hello@metiscode.com](mailto:hello@metiscode.com) is sufficient.

## 8. Right to object

Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you may
object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.
